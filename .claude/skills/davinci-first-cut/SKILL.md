Edit the currently open DaVinci Resolve project into a polished first cut. Analyze the timeline and remove unnecessary pauses, awkward silences, false starts, mistakes, failed attempts, repeated takes, stumbles, and sections where the speaker clearly restarts or corrects themselves. Keep the edit natural and well-paced rather than aggressively cutting every pause.

After cleaning the edit, replace the existing voice with a natural-sounding voice generated using Qwen3-TTS. Preserve the original script, wording, timing, and intended delivery as closely as possible while making the replacement voice sound smooth and consistent throughout the video. Make sure the new voice is properly synchronized with the edited footage and does not introduce noticeable timing gaps or overlaps.

Maintain important context and intentional pauses. Do not remove useful reactions, emphasis, comedic timing, or information simply because there is silence. The goal is a clean, professional first cut that feels intentionally edited rather than artificially chopped up.

Use DaVinci Resolve directly and inspect the resulting timeline after making the edits. Ensure there are no accidental gaps, overlapping clips, broken audio, obvious jump cuts, or synchronization problems. Preserve the existing project structure, video assets, and overall creative intent unless changes are necessary to complete the edit.
